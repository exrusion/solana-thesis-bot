PROOF OF WORK

the reasoning is hashed on chain before the trade exists

Anyone can claim their bot decided before it bought. This page lets you check it instead. Before an order exists, the whole decision — the token, the verdict, every rule and the numbers behind it, plus a random nonce — is hashed with SHA-256, and only that hash is written into a Solana memo. A validator stamps it into a block at a time nobody here controls. Twenty minutes later the plaintext is published. Hash it yourself; it has to match the hash already sealed in an earlier block.

The memo is signed by a separate burner key that cannot sign a swap. The trades themselves are placed by code, not by hand — every fill below was composed, signed and sent by the bot with no human in the loop.
